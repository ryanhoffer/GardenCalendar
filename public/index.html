Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can plan your whole garden without signing up — your data stays on your device. Create a free account only when you want to sync across devices.

What do I get with Premium?

Premium adds secure cloud sync and backup, so your garden is available on your phone, tablet, and computer — and safe if you lose a device.

Where do frost dates and weather come from?

We use your ZIP code to look up location-specific frost estimates and pull daily weather history, so timing is tailored to where you actually garden.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings — you keep access through the end of your billing period.
